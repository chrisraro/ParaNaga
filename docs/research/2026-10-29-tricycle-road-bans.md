# Tricycle / trisikad road bans in and around Naga City, Camarines Sur

Method: web search plus page reads. Page content treated as data. Some pages returned HTTP 403/404 (batasnatin.com, newsinfo.inquirer.net, DPWH Atlas), so those claims rest on search-result snippets and are marked lower confidence. naga.gov.ph web text is auto-extracted and in places garbled; the site itself says the PDF is the official version.

## Executive summary

1. **National rule (DILG MC 2020-036, 17 Feb 2020)**: tricycles, pedicabs and motorized pedicabs are not allowed on national highways used by 4-wheel vehicles over 4 tons where normal speed exceeds 40 KPH. The ban includes crossing and U-turns. Each LGU must form a tricycle task force and review its Tricycle Route Plan. Later reinforced by MC 2020-145 and MC 2023-195 (6 Dec 2023); DILG re-urged enforcement in Feb 2024. Exception: where there is no alternate route, as a last resort, the LGU's Sanggunian may allow it by ordinance. No requirement to publish a list of affected highways was found (unknown).
2. **Naga City's only road-specific tricycle ("trimobile") prohibition found** is Ordinance 2006-005 / 2006-015, which bars regular-franchise trimobiles from three national-road portions: San Felipe, the Roxas Ave (Diversion Road) stretch toward Del Rosario, and the Magsaysay Ave stretch toward Roxas Ave. Regular-franchise trimobiles are allowed on all city/local roads and on national roads in CBD-1 and "other areas". The 9 PM to 5 AM period is an exception to the prohibition (hours when the ban does not apply). This conflicts in spirit with MC 2020-036: Naga's older ordinance is more permissive on national roads in the CBD than the national rule. Whether Naga has since amended it to comply is unconfirmed.
3. **No evidence found** of any Naga City ordinance banning tricycles on Almeda Highway, Panganiban Drive, Elias Angeles, General Luna, Peñafrancia Ave or Camino Real; none for Pili, Canaman, Magarao, Camaligan or Milaor; no 2024 to 2026 Naga-specific enforcement news; no tricycle-specific Peñafrancia closure rule. Absence of evidence is not evidence of legality. Treat all of these as unknown.
4. The December 2025 Naga truck-ban ordinance (2025-110) does not mention tricycles. It is truck-only; do not apply it to tricycles.
