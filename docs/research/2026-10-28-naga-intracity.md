**Fares (VERIFIED)**

  - traditional: ₱14 for the first 4 km, then ₱2/km;
  - modern: ₱17 for the first 4 km, then ₱2.40/km.
- History: approved 17 Mar 2026, suspended 18 Mar, and the suspension lifted by a DOTr memo of 25 Sep 2026.

**Fare guide expiry (UNVERIFIED)**
- LTFRB says the temporary fare guide is valid until **31 Dec 2026**. From then on, the official guide or matrix must be displayed.
- No order number was found, and ltfrb.gov.ph returned 403.

**Tricycles**

**Disruption**

## Tricycles, e-trikes and padyak

**Fares**
- Ordinance 2024-002 (16 Jan 2024): ₱15 for trimobiles, e-trikes and taxicles, with a 20% discount for seniors, students, PWD and solo parents on showing an ID. PSO prepares the per-route matrix (N13).
- The ordinance does not say how many km the fare covers. The 2022 ₱13 rate covered the first 3 km.
- Per-km and special-trip rates: NOT FOUND.
- No 2025–2026 fare ordinance was found (N19–N21).
- EASTTODA (22 Jun 2026) charges ₱15 regular and ₱13 student. It raised capacity from 4 to 5 passengers without an ordinance (N15, UNVERIFIED).
- Padyak: ₱10 regular / ₱8 discounted (2022) is still the latest found.

**Ordinances**
- **2025-056** (5 Aug 2025): unified VISTODA/LAVISTODA route with 30 units. It runs Langon ↔ Ateneo via Vistansa, and Centro Cararayan ↔ San Miguel St / Tecla San Andres-Ziga Rd via Salunguigui. Units may not enter Tecla San Andres-Ziga Rd. It repeals 2025-023 (N22). Correction: the baseline cites 2025-067 for this.
- **2026-024** (24 Feb 2026): one extra padyak unit each for NACISUPODA, TAPODA, COPPODA, CALIPODA and St. James (N23).
- **2025-128** (19 Dec 2025): e-bikes and e-trikes must be registered with LTO, and may not be used for hire without a franchise. It lists national roads, including Maharlika, Almeda, Roxas, Magsaysay, M.T. Villanueva and Pacol–Panicuason (N24).
- **2026-023** (24 Feb 2026): slow-moving vehicle lane. Trikes, e-trikes and stopping PUVs use the outermost lane. Penalties: warning, then ₱500, then ₱1,000 plus franchise suspension (N25).
- **2026-062** (26 May 2026): citizens can report violations with photos through MyNaga or CCTV, including improper loading and not following designated routes (N26).

## Terminals

- **Eastbound Terminal (Triangulo):**
  - Opened 6 Nov 2021; operated by Ruby Shelter; 1,515 m²; serves Rinconada and Partido routes (N27).
  - The trial places it on Anciano St (N02).
  - Ordinance 2022-013 (a temporary CBD II eastbound terminal) sets dispatch fees of ₱40 per jeepney and ₱60 per van (UNVERIFIED snippet).
- **Bicol Central Station:**
  - Ninoy & Cory Ave, Triangulo.
  - Ordinance 2024-032 sets new fees; see the intercity report for its content.
- **Southbound (Sabang/LCC) and Northbound (Queborac):** both had three-year franchises. Renewal: NOT FOUND.
- **M.T. Villanueva Ave:**
  - 800 m, Peñafrancia → Queborac.
  - DPWH works started Oct 2026; cable excavation from 5 Nov 2026; completion about Apr–May 2027.
  - Lay-bys will be for loading and unloading only (N14, 14 Oct 2026).
- **Not found:** city bus, shuttle, libreng sakay, in-city UV Express.
