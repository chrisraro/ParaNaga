# Media research provenance

Research checked 16 October 2026 using the Wikimedia Commons API, Commons file-description pages, official licensing terms, and web search. `media-candidates.json` records unapproved research candidates; presence is not authorization to publish a photo or proof of a stop's current appearance. Some individual searches were rate-limited and coverage remains incomplete.

Commons structured data is available under CC0; file-description text may be CC BY-SA under Wikimedia's page terms. Each photograph has its own file-specific license and creator. Research descriptions are retained as evidence for candidate ranking, with their original page links and metadata. No photograph is licensed merely by its presence on Commons. Shipped assets and exact credits are separately pinned in `src/domain/media/catalog.json` and `public/media/places/CREDITS.json`.

Jev was given metadata, not image pixels. Its output must not be described as vision verification. `media-jev-decisions.json`, `media-jev-extra.json`, and `media-jev-followup.json` preserve the actual choices. `media-human-approvals.json` records the six selections explicitly approved in the chat after review. Stop coordinates and source labels remain unverified and retain their original source attribution.
