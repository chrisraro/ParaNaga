# Naga City intercity public transport (travel out of Naga), 2026-10-28

> **AI-written draft. This is evidence, not app data.** Nothing here goes into the app until a reviewer checks it and publishes it. Every web page was treated as untrusted.

**Confidence levels used below:**
- **High:** an official source, or two or more independent ones.
- **Medium:** one reputable source.
- **Low / UNVERIFIED:** one source, a booking site, or an undated page.

## Summary

**Rail**
- The only Naga train running is **PNR Naga–Lupi Viejo**: 3 trips a day each way, about 1h39m end to end, ₱15–₱75, since 2025-11-05 (Medium-High).
- **Naga–Legazpi is suspended** since 2025-11-10 because of typhoon damage to a bridge at Guinobatan. There is no new reopening date (High).
- The **South Long Haul is planned only and unfunded** (High).

**Bus**
- All buses use **Bicol Central Station (BCS)**.
- Ordinance 2024-032 names the provincial routes: Caramoan, Garchitorena, Rinconada, Partido, Legazpi and Bulan (High).
- Manila operators: DLTB, Peñafrancia, Isarog, Raymond, ALPS and Legaspi St Jude.
- Philtranco announced it would stop on 2026-03-30; no resumption was found.

**Fares**
- LTFRB provisional provincial bus rates are in force from **2026-09-28**: ordinary ₱12 for the first 5 km, then ₱2.20/km; aircon ₱2.45/km (Medium-High).
- Fares for individual routes and operators come only from booking sites (Low).

**Vans and inter-town jeepneys**
- Terminals are documented.
- Fares are mostly not found or undated (Low).

**Air**
- Naga Airport (WNP, in Pili) has one service: Cebgo daily to **Clark**, since 2026-03-29 (Medium-High).

## 5. Air and sea

**Air**
- Naga Airport (WNP) is at San Jose, Pili, on Maharlika Hwy, and is run by CAAP.
- Cebgo flies Clark–Naga daily (DG6129/6130) since 2026-03-29, replacing the Manila flights DG6113/6114 (R30–R32).
- No PAL service was found.
- Getting there: walk about 400 m to the Maharlika Highway and take a Naga-bound jeepney or bus. The fare was not found.

**Sea**
- Caramoan: van to Sabang port (San Jose), then a boat to Guijalo. About ₱120 for the boat, from an old source (Low).
- Catanduanes: van to Tabaco, then a ferry (2023 listing, Low).
- Matnog: reached by buses passing through. No trip starting in Naga was confirmed.
